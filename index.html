About Us

Kavish Enterprises is a Navi Mumbai based transportation company specializing in Fuel, Oil, Chemical and Petrochemical logistics across India.


We operate a well-maintained fleet of tankers equipped with 35KL and 40KL stainless steel tanks, ensuring safe, efficient, and reliable long-distance transportation across India.


We focus on timely delivery, safety compliance, and professional service standards. Our operations cover major industrial routes including Mumbai, Gujarat, South India, North India, and Eastern India.


With a strong network of experienced drivers and transport coordination, we ensure smooth operations, quick load execution, and consistent service reliability.
