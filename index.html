About Us

Kavish Enterprises is a Navi Mumbai based transportation company specializing in Fuel, Oil, Chemical and Petrochemical logistics across India.


We operate a modern fleet of 14-tyre Ashok Leyland tankers with 35KL & 40KL stainless steel tanks, ensuring safe, reliable and efficient long-distance transportation.


Our focus is on timely delivery, safety compliance and professional service standards. We handle bulk liquid transportation for industries across Mumbai, Gujarat, South India, North India and Eastern India.


With a strong network of drivers and transport partners, we ensure smooth operations, quick load execution and consistent service reliability.
